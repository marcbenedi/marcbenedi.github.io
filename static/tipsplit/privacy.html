Privacy Policy for Tip & Split

Last updated: 6 July 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Tip & Split mobile application (the “App”) for Android, published by Marc Benedi.

Data we collect

None. Tip & Split does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage data. The App has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and requires no permissions.

How your information is used

Any values you enter (bill amount, tip percentage, number of people) are used only to perform the calculation on your device and are held in memory while the App is open. They are never saved, uploaded, or sent anywhere.

Internet access

The App works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.

Third parties

The App contains no third-party SDKs, trackers, or advertising networks.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised “Last updated” date.
