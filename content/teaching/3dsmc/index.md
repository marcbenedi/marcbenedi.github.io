Teaching Assistant for **3D Scanning & Motion Capture (IN2354)** at the Technical University of Munich, taught by Prof. Matthias Nießner.

The course covers the foundations of acquiring and reconstructing 3D geometry and motion from sensor data. Topics include depth sensing and RGB-D cameras, camera calibration, point cloud processing, rigid and non-rigid registration (ICP and variants), volumetric surface reconstruction with signed distance functions (KinectFusion-style fusion and meshing), as well as motion capture, skeletal tracking, and parametric human body models (e.g. SMPL). Lectures are paired with programming exercises in C++ where students implement the core pipeline pieces themselves.

Semesters taught:

- Winter Semester 2023/24
- Summer Semester 2024
- Winter Semester 2024/25
- Summer Semester 2025
- Winter Semester 2025/26
- Summer Semester 2026
